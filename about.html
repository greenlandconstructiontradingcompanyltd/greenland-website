About the company

Greenland Construction and Trading Company is registered in Somalia under number CPR/2010/21160. The head office is in Mogadishu, with sub-offices in Baidoa and Wajid, and operations cover southern Somalia.

The company builds and rehabilitates buildings and water infrastructure, supplies materials and equipment, and provides transport and logistics. Its stated approach is affordable, quality services and long-term relationships with clients.

Vision

To be a premier enterprise in the construction and supply industry, focused on available opportunities within Somalia and beyond.

Mission

To provide quality and affordable construction and supply services to our clients.
